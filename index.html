About me

Legal knowledge with a management perspective.

Udhab Dahal has worked in the legal profession for more than 12 years, handling matters across different areas of law. With over 1,000 cases completed, he brings broad practical experience to legal challenges faced by individuals, cooperatives, and businesses.

His work includes supporting organisations through business formation and liquidation, as well as providing legal advice to cooperatives and working with NGOs and INGOs. Alongside his legal practice, he applies management knowledge to help clients understand the legal and organisational dimensions of their decisions.
